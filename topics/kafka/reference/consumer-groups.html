Consumer groups

Rules worth memorising

  • Within a group, each partition has exactly one consumer. One consumer may own many partitions.
  • Maximum parallelism equals partition count. Extra consumers sit idle.
  • Different groups each read every event, independently.
  • Same group.id shares the work. Different group.ids each get a full copy of the stream.

What triggers a rebalance

  • A member joins the group.
  • A member leaves or fails.
  • A subscribed topic's partition count changes.
  • A subscribed topic is created or deleted.

Range assignor

Per topic: partitions in numeric order, consumers in sorted order, and any remainder goes to the first consumers. With 2 consumers and 2 topics of 3 partitions each, the split is 4 and 2.
